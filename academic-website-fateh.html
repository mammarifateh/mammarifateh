Welcome to my personal academic website! I am Fateh Mammari, a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering specializing in Surface Elasticity Theory, Nanoplates, and Thin Film Mechanics. My research focuses on the mechanical behavior of materials at the nanoscale, particularly using non-classical elasticity theories to model and characterize thin structures.
